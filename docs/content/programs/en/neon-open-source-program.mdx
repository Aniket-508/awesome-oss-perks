## Perks

- **Platform Credits**: Eligible open-source software receives up to $5,000 in platform credits yearly.
- **Cash Referral Program**: Receive $20 via GitHub Sponsorships for every developer who discovers Neon through your work and becomes a customer.
- **Marketing Support**: Promotional support to expand your project's visibility to Neon's 1M+ developer community through blog posts, social media, case studies, and co-marketing initiatives.

## Eligibility

- Your work must be open source and publicly available under a recognized open-source license.
- Developers should be able to self-host the software.
- Open-source software that uses Postgres is an ideal fit.
- Provide documentation, CLI tools, and code examples that teach users how to build with your software and Neon.

## How to apply

1. Get in touch via the contact form.
2. Wait for a response, typically within 1-3 business days.
